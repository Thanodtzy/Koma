Our story

Three friends. One passion. A coffee culture worth sharing.

COMA Coffee was born from long conversations, curious experiments, and the simple joy of coffee with friends. We believe that great coffee is not only about flavor, but also about the people, stories, and moments behind it.

What began as a small habit slowly became a dream: to create a welcoming place where every cup feels personal, warm, and meaningful.
